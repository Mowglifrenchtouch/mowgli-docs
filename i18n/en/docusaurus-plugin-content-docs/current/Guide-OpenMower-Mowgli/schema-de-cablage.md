## 🧩 Important Details

- The diagram shows connections to essential peripherals: IMU, GPS, Pi, power supplies, etc.
- Wire colours follow commonly used conventions.

- The 500B models require an adaptation on the physical UART port and therefore a firmware modification to function — see the dedicated section on [installation & configuration](/docs/Guide-OpenMower-Mowgli/installation-preparation-pi).
