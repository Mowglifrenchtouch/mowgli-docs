## 🧩 Juditech3D's Creations

### 🔧 Modular Support V2 — Raspberry Pi + GPS + Power Supply

Modular system designed to easily integrate electronic components into the Yardforce 500B.

**Features:**
- Full modularity — adapt to your specific needs
- Compatible: Raspberry Pi 3/4, ZED-F9P / Ardusimple boards, UM960, UM980/982, DC/DC power supplies
- Screw or insert fixing
- Interchangeable modules between RPi and GPS brackets
- Blank customisable version included

| Module | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Raspberry Pi 3/4 bracket | Screw or insert fixing |
| GPS board bracket | Ardusimple + compatible, with UM960/UM980/982 adapter |
| DC/DC LM2596 power bracket | Screw or insert fixing |
| DC/DC XL4016E1 power bracket | Screw or insert fixing |
| DC/DC XL4015 power bracket | Screw or insert fixing |
| DC/DC USB power bracket | — |
| Blank module | Customisable to your needs |

🔗 [V1 Model](https://makerworld.com/models/603899) — V2 coming soon

## 🧩 Pepeuch's Creations

### 📦 RPi + GNSS + DC/DC + F9P complete support kit

All-in-one kit for Yardforce 500B:
- Raspberry Pi
- uBlox F9P (UART and USB)
- DC/DC 5A
- GNSS HA901A antenna inside the chassis (`X=0.295`, `Y=0`)
- 2mm and 4mm shim washers

🔗 [MakerWorld Model](https://makerworld.com/fr/models/1362541-bloc-support-rpi-gnss-dcdc-f9p-yardforce-500b#profileId-1407557)

---

### ⚙️ SA motor adapter → Classic 500B chassis

Adapter to mount SA series wheel motors (more reliable gearboxes) onto the Classic 500B chassis.

:::warning Irreversible modification
This modification **voids the warranty** — you will need to drill the chassis with a Ø 40mm hole saw.
:::

Recommended printing in **ABS or PETG** (PLA possible but not recommended).

🔗 [MakerWorld Model](https://makerworld.com/en/models/1337197-yarforce-motor-adapter-sa-on-classic-500b#profileId-1376751)
